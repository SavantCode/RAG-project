# 🏗️ Enterprise Multi-Modal & Multi-Sheet RAG — Architecture & Deep-Dive Guide

This document provides an exhaustive, file-by-file and stage-by-stage technical breakdown of how the **Enterprise Hybrid RAG System** works under the hood. It covers the system architecture, dual-database storage topology, ingestion pipelines for **Excel/CSV**, **PDF/DOCX/TXT**, and **Live Website URLs**, and the **5-Stage Hybrid Retrieval & Conversational LLM Flow**.
